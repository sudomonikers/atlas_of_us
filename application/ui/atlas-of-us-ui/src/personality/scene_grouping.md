# Scene Grouping

150 IPIP-NEO items, 5 per facet, grouped into 18 scenes. Each item is in exactly one scene, and every facet is spread over at least two. The full 300 items, with the IDs used here, are in `ipip_neo_items.md` in this folder.

## How to read this
- **Item IDs:** the facet, then the item's place in that facet's list in `ipip_neo_items.md`. `N1.10` is Anxiety's 10th item, "Adapt easily to new situations."
- **+ / −:** agreeing with the item scores the facet higher (+) or lower (−). The scene should give room for both ends to look reasonable.
- **★:** the item is also in the IPIP-NEO-120 short form.
- **Modes:**
  - **Story:** a hypothetical with choices and free-text whys.
  - **Live:** we record what the player actually does in the app, such as what they open, how long they spend, and what they skip.
  - **Interview:** we ask directly, but every frequency word is pinned down. "Often" becomes "on how many of the last 30 days". Use this for inner states and habits a single situation can't show.
- **Beats** are moments in a scene. One prompt can test several items at once.

## Which 5 items per facet
- **Four are the IPIP-NEO-120's,** the best-validated items for each facet (Johnson, 2014).
- **The fifth is one more from the 300.** It's mostly picked so each facet has at least one item keyed in the other direction, or because it works especially well in a scene.
- **Liberalism is the exception.**
  - The 120's two voting items ("Tend to vote for liberal / conservative political candidates") are dropped.
  - The trait itself is global: being willing to question authority, tradition and fixed moral rules.
  - The party labels aren't. "Liberal" means the centre-right in Australia and free-market in much of Europe.
  - So we keep five items that test the attitude without the labels.

## Order
18 scenes, scored in three batches of 6 by play order (1–6, 7–12, 13–18), and each batch is read in one call. So the order does two jobs:
- **Give every batch a good mix of facets.** Each batch should touch as many of the 30 as it can, so every read sees all five domains rather than one or two. This order covers 23, 25 and 24 facets per batch, where the old one covered 20 in each.
- **Open light and playful.** The Story Game has low stakes and is fun to play, so players learn the loop before anything gets hard. The Night Class follows with a puzzle and a lively room, and The Bad Day closes the first batch with comedy. The heavy scenes (The Shoplifter, The Promotion, The Stranger, The Feud) wait for the second and third batches.
- **Mix the middle.** Action follows reflection, and each of the first two batches ends light (The Bad Day, The Festival), so two heavy scenes don't sit side by side.
- **Save the heaviest for the end.** The Cabin's long wait and The Portrait's mood questions come once the player is invested and trusts the app.
- **Close with The Portrait.** It's reflective and pays off by showing the player a picture of themselves. It's also where the "how often" interview questions sit.
