# QA round 3: "Sam"

## The persona
Sam is 52 and teaches art at a secondary school. Sam paints in the shed at weekends, and has a few close friends rather than a crowd.

**What Sam is like:**
- **Open:** a daydreamer who invents stories about strangers on the bus and is moved by beauty. Sam spent a year painting in Portugal on the money from selling a flat.
- **Warm:** trusting, kind and forgiving, and thinks rules are there to help people.
- **Steady:** calm in a crisis, rarely anxious or self-conscious, and quick to recover.
- **Disorganised:** not a planner, late with paperwork, and unbothered by flaws.
- **Not competitive:** cheerful and playful, with no interest in the spotlight.

**Why this persona:**
- **A third, different shape:** Ruth (round 1) was an anxious, rule-bound introvert, and Dev (round 2) was bold, anxious and competitive.
- **Weight on openness:** Sam puts it on the traits the new Owambe scene was built for (Imagination, Artistic Interests, Liberalism, Cheerfulness, Optimism and Playfulness), and on low Neuroticism, which neither earlier persona had.
- **Answer style:** medium length and natural, somewhere between Ruth's essays and Dev's one-liners.

**What it checks:**
- **The Owambe and the two new Water prompts:** do Artistic Interests, Liberalism, Patience and Sensory Sensitivity now get enough evidence?
- **The new Anxiety rule:** Sam is low on Anxiety, and the rule now stops calm choices counting as evidence. Does a calm person still score low, from what Sam says?
- **The new Emotionality rule:** Sam talks about feelings in real-life answers (grief, crying) but not much in the story. Does Emotionality come only from those?
- **The Archive's clock:** the first round where it runs out. Sam lingers over the letter, sketchbook and orrery, never reaches the last crate, and has "no regrets".
- **Middle values:** Gregariousness, Assertiveness, Boldness, Intellect, Excitement-Seeking and Decisiveness should stay in the middle.

**How the targets were set:** following round 2's lesson, these targets were set after writing the answers, from what the answers actually show.

## Analysis

### What worked
- **The Owambe hit every trait it was built for.**

  | Trait | Score | Target |
  |---|---|---|
  | Imagination | 0.90 | 0.90 |
  | Artistic Interests | 0.92 | 0.90 |
  | Liberalism | 0.85 | 0.80 |
  | Cheerfulness | 0.85 | 0.85 |
  | Optimism | 0.85 | 0.85 |
  | Playfulness | 0.84 | 0.80 |

  - Liberalism had a total weight of 1.1 in round 2. It now has 4.1.
- **Patience** came out at 0.81 against a target of 0.80. The new `three_days` prompt in The Water supplied the evidence.
- **Low Neuroticism scores well:**

  | Trait | Score | Target |
  |---|---|---|
  | Anxiety | 0.26 | 0.15 |
  | Vulnerability | 0.13 | 0.20 |
  | Anger | 0.17 | 0.15 |
  | Self-Consciousness | 0.25 | 0.15 |

  - These came from what Sam says ("delighted", "I'd rather ask than stew", "I go quite calm"), not from calm choices.
- **Gregariousness stayed in the middle:** 0.52 against a target of 0.55. Sam goes out but prefers a few friends, and the evidence balanced out.
- **The Archive's countdown works:**
  - Scoring started on the last question, even though the last crate was never reached.
  - "No regrets" was scored as Resilience and Optimism.
  - Lingering over the letter, the sketchbook and the orrery was scored as low Self-Discipline, with high Intellect and Artistic Interests.

### Where it missed, and why

1. **Bug: answers from a branch not taken on a replay are still scored.**
   - **The two stale answers:**
     - Dev's "120." to the captain is now on Sam's record as Morality −0.9 and Dutifulness −0.8. Sam asked the passengers instead, and Mr Doyle gave up his place.
     - Dev's "unsorted" crate scored Perfectionism −0.7. Sam's clock ran out before the last crate.
   - **Why it happens:** `answer_question` only ever adds or replaces answers. Replaying a scene down a different branch, or running out of time sooner, leaves the earlier answers in the database, and scoring sends every answer to the scorer. It even re-scores them against the new playthrough.
   - **Real users are affected:** anyone who replays a scene and makes different choices gets scored partly on their old run.
   - **Fix:** when a scene's first question is answered, delete that person's other answers in the scene. Every scene's first question comes before any branch, so answering it means a fresh run.
   - **Effect on this round's error:** small (0.132 with them, 0.135 without), but they put a lie on the wrong person's record.

2. **Rule ignored: the scorer still scores Emotionality from the story.**
   - Emotionality came out at 0.90 against a target of 0.75.
   - The top evidence was mostly in-story:
     - "It undid me a bit" about the old man on the train (+0.9).
     - Getting teary at midnight (+0.8).
     - The hurt in the wings (+0.8).
   - The rule says to score it only from real-life answers. The scorer reads Sam's articulate in-story feelings as exactly what the trait describes, and ignores the restriction.
   - Sam really is emotionally open, so the direction is right, but the prompt rule clearly isn't working.
   - **Fix:** enforce it in code. In `recombine`, drop Emotionality scores from answers that aren't real-life. That also stops it rising for everyone who answers feelings questions.

3. **Sensory Sensitivity was misread:** 0.70 against a target of 0.30.
   - "I can sleep through most things… the rain on the roof is quite soothing" was scored +0.3, as noticing different noises. It should have scored strongly low.
   - Noticing Juno's body language was also scored high, because the description includes "subtle cues". That makes it an empathy trait as well as a sensory one.
   - **Fix:** drop "and subtle cues" from the description, and add "noticing people's feelings is Sympathy" to the neighbouring-traits rule.

4. **Stubbornness reads high on principled people.**
   - Results so far: Ruth 0.82 against 0.30, Dev 0.78 against 0.70, Sam 0.74 against 0.25.
   - The answers driving it:
     - "No, find somebody else" to Marvello.
     - Holding to the truth against Pru, in both Ruth's and Sam's runs.
   - That is literally holding a position under pressure, but it's integrity, not stubbornness.
   - **Fix:** change the description to "holding to one's position or course despite good reasons to change". Refusing to back down from the truth or one's values isn't Stubbornness.

5. **Misreading where an answer comes from.**
   - **Story taken as the person:** "I'd never have gone so loud on it" was scored as high Assertiveness, citing the signatures and group-chat campaign. Those are the scene's backstory, not Sam's.
   - **Curiosity taken as craving:** reading the letter and the grandmother's letters were scored as Immoderation, which is about cravings and urges. Immoderation came out at 0.62 against a target of 0.35.
   - **Fix:** add "curiosity is Intellect or Adventurousness, not Immoderation" to the neighbouring-traits rule.

6. **Some targets were mine to get wrong again, despite setting them after the answers.**
   - **Boldness** (0.91 against 0.55): Sam apologised in front of fifty people, sold a flat to paint in Portugal, stayed behind in the flood, and went to Mars. Under a definition that includes moral courage, that is bold.
   - **Assertiveness** (0.78 against 0.45): Sam asked a colleague directly, corrected the record with Ms. Kuroda, and started the sing-along. Gentle, but direct.
   - **Cautiousness** (0.60 against 0.35): Sam rationed the water, waited for the kiln, had a splash at the toast, and read the Mars contract first.
   - **Perfectionism** (0.59 against 0.15): I wrote Sam saying "I'm too precious about the good ones".
   - **The lesson:** I keep picturing the persona, not reading the answers. The fix for the next round is a second pass, scoring each target from the answer text alone.

7. **Boldness is becoming a catch-all for any costly good deed.** Sam scored 0.91 and Dev 0.92, with opposite personalities.
   - Most of the evidence for both is doing the right thing at a cost: telling the truth, apologising, staying behind to help.
   - Including moral courage makes Boldness move with Morality and Altruism.
   - This is the trade-off chosen in round 1, but three rounds show that it stops Boldness telling people apart.
   - **Fix:** take moral courage back out, and keep Boldness to social and physical risk.

### Recommendations (not done)
1. **Fix the replay bug.** This is the only change that affects real users today. When a scene's first question is answered, delete the person's other answers in that scene.
2. **Enforce Emotionality in code.** In `recombine`, ignore Emotionality scores from answers that aren't real-life.
3. **Change two descriptions:**
   - **Sensory Sensitivity:** drop "and subtle cues".
   - **Stubbornness:** add "despite good reasons to change".
4. **Reverse round 1's Boldness decision:** social and physical risk only. Doing the right thing despite fear is evidence for whatever made it right.
5. **Add to the neighbouring-traits rule:**
   - Curiosity is not Immoderation.
   - Noticing feelings is Sympathy, not Sensory Sensitivity.
   - The scene's backstory isn't the person's.
6. **Testing:** before running, have a second pass score each target from the answer text alone.

## Changes made as a result (14 Oct 2026)
Recommendations 1, 3, 4 and 5 were made. Recommendation 2 (enforcing Emotionality in code) wasn't. To be tested in round 4.

**1. Replay bug fixed** (`answer_question` in `apis/atlas-api/src/personality/mod.rs`, finding 1)
- **The change:** answering a scene's first question now deletes the person's other answers to that scene, in the same query that saves the answer.
- **Why it's safe:** every scene's order-0 question comes before any branch, so answering it always means a fresh run. This was checked against all 14 scripts; The Thief's opening recall sections all lead to `first_suspicion` before any question.
- **Side effect:** a finished scene shows as in progress again as soon as a replay starts. That's correct.
- **Existing data:** stale answers already in the database stay until that scene is replayed. On the test account, round 4's first answers will clear them.

**3. Descriptions** (`database/personality_seed.cypher`; the Explore overviews in `personality_explore_seed.cypher` match)
- **Sensory Sensitivity** is now "Being strongly affected by noise, light, crowds and other sensations." "Subtle cues" was dropped (finding 3).
- **Stubbornness** is now "Holding to one's position or course despite pressure and good reasons to change." (finding 4)

**4. Boldness reversed** (finding 7)
- **Description:** now "Taking social or physical risks despite fear, for something one wants."
- **Scoring rule:** doing the right thing despite fear now counts as evidence for whatever made it right (Morality, Altruism), not Boldness. This undoes round 1's decision.

**5. Scoring rules** (`SCORING_PROMPT` in `apis/atlas-jobs/src/personality.rs`)
- **The story bullet:** now says that nothing the scene says the person did before it began is evidence about them (finding 5).
- **Keeping neighbouring traits apart:** three new distinctions.
  - **Immoderation** (finding 5): curiosity is never Immoderation, which is cravings and urges like food, drink and spending.
  - **Sensory Sensitivity** (finding 3): noticing how other people feel is Sympathy, not Sensory Sensitivity.
  - **Stubbornness** (finding 4): holding to the truth or one's values under pressure is not Stubbornness.
